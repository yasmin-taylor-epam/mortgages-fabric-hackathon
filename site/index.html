Shared core: three clocks

Proposed target

The blueprint uses three timing targets. A live event and an ML score refresh are separate operations. The demo shows and measures both.

60 s

From a live event to its appearance in the Customer 360 view.

15 min

Between scoring runs that refresh features, assign segments and calculate refinancing risk scores. These runs do not retrain the models.

5 min

From a successful scoring run to the retention alert.

  • Measure ingestion, view refresh, scoring and alert delivery separately. These are targets to test, not platform guarantees.
  • A scheduled Lakehouse export alone is not a real-time view. Live events go through Eventstream into Eventhouse.
  • A notebook started by hand is a test drill. The final design runs scoring on a schedule.
  • A failed scoring run leaves the last score marked stale. The live view still updates.

Do not claim more than the evidence shows

“90 days early”

Calculate the score 90 days before renewal. Freeze the information available at that checkpoint. Evaluate the later renewal outcome. Predicting churn "within 90 days" does not establish 90 days of warning. If the warning comes later, report the failed target.

“We know why”

Feature attributions explain how model inputs affect the score. Checked events support investigation. Neither proves the customer's true motivation. Neither proves that a retention action will work.

“We saved revenue”

A demo can show recurring revenue at risk and recorded actions. Proving improved retention requires evidence from real retention actions and a comparison group. Synthetic results prove only that the process works.

Show the proof

What the demo and video must show.

  • Test on a later time period that training excludes. Do not use information from after each feature snapshot.
  • Compare the model with a simple rules baseline.
  • Measure precision: the share of flagged customers that churn. Measure recall: the share of churned customers that the model flags.
  • Evaluate precision and recall at the number of customers the team can contact.
  • Measure lead time for each confirmed leaver: outcome_at minus first_eligible_warning_at. Report the distribution.
  • Show one live event that creates exactly one retention case. Keep a record that supports an audit.
  • Show a healthy or seasonal customer, not only the scripted failure.
  • Demonstrate ML segments and alert-routing rules separately. Do not label rules as ML.

Access, Fabric capacity, alert integration, team availability and the dataset's source remain unconfirmed. Check these before promising the final stack.

How one warning becomes one action

Company, customer and counterexample (illustrative)

The company and customer

HomeBridge is a fictional mortgage lender. It services repayment mortgages and offers product transfers when fixed-rate deals end.

The customer unit is one mortgage account, not an individual borrower. Joint borrowers remain linked parties, not duplicate customers. A servicing adviser owns each renewal review.

The problem and counterexample

Score external refinancing risk at the checkpoint, 90 days before the fixed-rate deal ends. Payment-support needs take priority over commercial retention reviews.

Alex reports concern about future payments while the renewal portal repeatedly fails during a product-transfer request. The adviser must inspect both problems.

Sam completes a product transfer without payment problems. A quiet renewal-portal history alone does not justify an alert.

Build specification at a glance

The rules that must hold, the data shape, and the failure cases the demo must pass. Full detail is in the sections below.

Invariants

  1. INV-1An active payment-support request routes to trained servicing staff first, whatever the churn score.
  2. INV-2Features use only data known at the checkpoint. Both occurred_at and ingested_at must be at or before cutoff_at.
  3. INV-3At most one open internal review case exists per mortgage_id + cycle_id + action_family.
  4. INV-4An unknown or incomplete refinancing outcome gets no model label. It never counts as retained.
  5. INV-5risk_updated fires only after a scoring run succeeds. A failed run leaves the last score marked stale.
  6. INV-6The prototype sends no customer messages. It makes no rate or credit decisions.

Data contract

Mortgage account
One loan account and fixed-rate deal cycle.
Payment
One posted ledger change.
Transfer request
One product-transfer request state change.
Servicing history
Source case state changes and dated contacts linked to each case. These are separate from a new review case.
Feature snapshot
One mortgage, deal cycle and cutoff time. The observed refinancing outcome stays separate.
Segment assignment
One mortgage account, deal cycle and scoring run.

Key: mortgage_id + cycle_id. The cycle identifies a fixed-rate deal, not a scoring run. Every event carries event time and ingestion time. The risk_updated event carries snapshot, model version, refinancing risk score, segment, evidence IDs and score time.

Failure cases the demo must pass

IDCaseExpected result
T01The same eligible event is replayed.One review case for the same case key.
T02An unknown account or a conflicting borrower mapping arrives.The record goes to quarantine.
T03The payment feed is stale or missing.The view flags the feed as stale. A missing feed is not zero arrears.
T04A payment is reversed.Review and rescoring start. The audit record stays.
T05A scoring run fails.The last score is marked stale. The live view still updates. No new risk_updated event fires. Service alerts remain independent.
T06A review-case write fails.The write retries with the same key. No duplicate review case appears.
T07Three contacts concern one unresolved problem. Three other contacts concern separate enquiries.The history distinguishes repeat contacts about one case from contacts about separate cases. A case state change alone does not count as a contact.

Deliberately not specified

  • Alert thresholds. The team freezes them on validation data.
  • Segment names. They follow inspection of the clusters.
  • Mortgage default prediction. This build predicts external refinancing, not failure to repay the loan.
  • Contact scripts and customer messages.
  • Fabric capacity and licences.

01 / Submission summary
  • Title: Mortgage Renewal Early Warning.
  • Problem: HomeBridge finds out about external refinancing only after the borrower leaves.
  • Solution: Fabric joins mortgage, payment, product-transfer request and servicing history with live renewal-portal events. It scores external refinancing risk at the checkpoint, 90 days before the fixed-rate deal ends. It explains each score to a servicing adviser.
  • Business impact: Advisers get time to arrange fixes for portal faults and review renewal needs before the borrower leaves. The 90-day lead time is a proposed target. Payment-support needs reach trained staff first.
  • Distinctive value: The design separates commercial loss from payment distress. A retained mortgage is not always a good customer outcome.
  • Scope: Fixed-rate repayment mortgage accounts, one renewal cohort, one renewal-portal fault replay and one payment-support case.
  • Out of scope: Lending decisions, rate changes, product recommendations and mortgage default prediction.
  • Form answers: The submission pack gives the text for each field of the blueprint form.

Support history shows the enquiry type, contacts per case and whether each problem is resolved or unresolved.

Synthetic data

03 / Architecture and interfaces

Keep the high-level design as the entry point. These four detailed views name components, stores, messages and failure paths.

Proposed implementation detail, not a deployed system. Table names and job boundaries are proposed. Tenant access and connector permissions remain unconfirmed.

Components Data relationships Scoring sequence Case routing

Processing steps

  1. Step 1Fabric pipelines load dated mortgage account, payment, product-transfer request and servicing exports into raw Lakehouse tables. See components
  2. Step 2Notebooks validate keys and ledger history. They build clean account histories and frozen feature snapshots. See components
  3. Step 3Notebooks train the renewal model and the behavioural clusters. MLflow records experiments, feature signatures and model versions. See data and evidence
  4. Step 4Scores, segments, attributions and evidence references go to curated Gold Delta tables. A completed-run marker makes them eligible for publication. A Power BI semantic model reads completed outputs in Direct Lake mode. See components
  5. Step 5Eventstream replays a renewal-portal software fault into Eventhouse. Each event keeps its original ID and timestamps. See components
  6. Step 6KQL journey queries in Eventhouse find three patterns: See components
    • each product-transfer request's stages and portal errors in time order
    • days that a request remains at one stage, for example while documents are outstanding or an offer is in review
    • retries after a renewal-portal software error.
  7. Step 7A Fabric pipeline copies versioned account context and completed score summaries into Eventhouse. KQL joins these copies with accepted live events. See components
  8. Step 8A separate pipeline copies cutoff-filtered KQL journey results into Lakehouse staging tables. Notebooks combine these results with dated history. See scoring sequence
  9. Step 9A Fabric pipeline runs scoring and segment assignment on a schedule. For the replay, the team also starts one scoring run by hand. See scoring sequence
  10. Step 10Activator and Power Automate create an internal review case, assigned to a person, in a secured SharePoint list. See case routing

Interfaces

  • risk_updated carries event_id, mortgage_id, cycle_id, run_id, snapshot_id, model_version, cluster_model_version, segment_id, scored_at, cutoff_at, target, score and evidence IDs.
  • A persisted publication record keeps the same event_id on retry. Activator passes the required fields to a Power Automate custom action.
  • The review-case key is mortgage_id + cycle_id + action_family. A duplicate write reads the existing review case. Other write failures stay visible.
  • The case writer checks current payment-support needs before creating a review case. Payment-support priority does not depend on refinancing risk or loan balance.

Capability sources and unresolved settings

Microsoft documents KQL database copy activities and Activator custom actions for Power Automate. These capabilities do not confirm tenant access.

  • Confirm context-copy frequency, live-view presentation and report refresh settings. The 60-second view target is not a Direct Lake guarantee.
  • Confirm retry limits, timeout values and exception ownership before the build. No automatic retry can bypass an eligibility check.
04 / Prediction and segments

Target

  • Checkpoint: deal_end_at minus 90 days.
  • Positive model label: confirmed external refinancing between the checkpoint and deal_end_at plus 30 days. Use known redemption reasons or verified refinance records.
  • Negative model label: a same-lender product transfer or continued servicing. Assign it only after the observation window ends, 30 days after deal end.
  • Excluded: sales, bereavement closures and redemptions with no known reason.

Features

  • Use product-transfer request completion, renewal-portal errors, complaint age, case category, contacts per case and case resolution status.
  • Support case status is resolved or unresolved at the checkpoint. A resolution recorded after the cutoff is not an input.
  • Derive three behavioural signals from dated history:
    • failed product-transfer request attempts in the last 30 days compared with the last 90 days
    • days that a request remains at one stage, for example while documents are outstanding or an offer is in review
    • support history: enquiry type, contacts per case, repeat contacts about unresolved problems and monthly contact trends.
  • Exclude sensitive support details, protected characteristics, names and fine-grained location. Check the remaining features for unjustified proxies.
  • Payment difficulty follows the payment-support policy. It is not a hidden external-refinancing outcome label.

Models and segments

  • Compare logistic regression and a small tree model with a product-transfer request-friction rules baseline.
  • Fit two to five clusters on product-transfer request and servicing features. Inspect stability and centroids before naming groups.
  • Fit clusters once per model release. Each scheduled run assigns accounts to those clusters, so a segment ID keeps its meaning.

Evaluation

  • Test on later deal cohorts. Keep their borrowers out of training.
  • Training outcome labels must exist before the simulated model release. Freeze thresholds on model-validation data.
  • Report accuracy, precision, recall, calibration and lift at ten reviews per run.
  • Lift compares the refinancing rate in the ten highest-scored accounts with the cohort's base rate. Accuracy alone misleads when few accounts leave.

Explanations

Store per-account feature attributions with each score. Use SHAP values for the tree model and coefficient contributions for logistic regression. They explain the score, not the borrower's motive.

05 / Alert routing and audit

Routing order

  1. An active payment-support request goes to trained servicing staff. Commercial renewal tasks wait while the payment-support case needs review.
  2. A verified renewal-portal software fault goes to technical support.
  3. An eligible external-refinancing risk candidate goes to a servicing adviser for a renewal review.

Eligibility here permits an internal review case. It does not approve a mortgage, a rate, or customer contact. A human checks contact policy and suitability before any customer-facing action.

Audit record

Every review case records evidence IDs, routing policy, assigned owner and decision. Score-based cases also record model version, run and snapshot. Record ingestion, scoring, alert and case-creation times separately where those operations apply.

Failure handling follows cases T01 to T06 above.

07 / Value and controls

Value measures

Measure review effort, renewal-portal faults fixed, payment-support routing time and confirmed external-refinancing outcomes. Loan balance is exposure, not saved revenue.

Data protection

  • Use pseudonymous accounts and least-privilege roles. Restrict support information separately.
  • Exclude bank details and borrower documents.
  • Apply Purview sensitivity labels to the Lakehouse, Eventhouse, semantic model and report.

Before real use

Get lending, privacy and responsible-AI review. Confirm jurisdiction, contact policy and fairness checks.
